Interleaf

Sealed mail between Obsidian vaults.

Your notes already live on your devices — your mail should too. Interleaf turns any vault into a mail endpoint: every message sealed to the recipient's key before it leaves, every signature checked against a pinned identity. Only the two vaults ever hold the key.

01

Verification is native. Every message carries a signature checked against a pinned key. Key changes are loud, not silent.

02

No accounts, no directory. First contact happens out-of-band through invite codes — the invite is the address. Strangers knock into a quarantine you review.

03

Relays can't read. The relay is a dumb per-fingerprint mailbox of opaque boxes. It sees routing metadata and ciphertext. That's all.

Security, concretely

·

Ed25519 keypair identity. Your address out there is a fingerprint; the seed is wrapped at rest and only exists in memory while unlocked.

·

NaCl sealed boxes. Every message signed with your key and encrypted to the recipient's before it leaves.

·

AES-256-GCM at rest. Message bodies sealed in the vault; Argon2id (memory-hard) wraps the vault key.

·

First contact is a knock. Strangers land in a quarantine you review — never straight into your inbox.

·

Key-change detection. A pinned contact announcing a new key is loud, not silent.

·

Safety numbers. Compare 30 digits out-of-band when identity matters.

·

Invite codes, QR, deep links. No directory to type an address into — the invite is the address.

·

No custom crypto. tweetnacl, @noble/hashes, and WebCrypto — all public, audited primitives.

Honest limits

  • Relay metadata is stated, not solved. The relay sees destination fingerprint, size, and timing for mail in flight — and stores only opaque ciphertext.
  • No ratchet, on purpose. Sealed boxes are static — no forward secrecy against a later long-term-key theft. The right bet for archival mail; Signal-style ratchets optimize a different threat.
  • First contact trusts the invite. Key pinning is TOFU: an intercepted first invite could swap keys. Quarantine plus a 30-second safety-number comparison closes that window.
  • Receipts never cross vaults. Remote conversations show no delivery or read receipts, by design.
  • A signature proves key possession, not personhood. Compare safety numbers out-of-band when it matters.
  • Relay retention is the outer bound. Mail in flight expires after 7 days; the relay must be up to receive it.
